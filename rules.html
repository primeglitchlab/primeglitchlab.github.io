Official Rules

“Myth or Truth?” weekly leaderboard & December Championship · Version 1 · last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

  • Free. No purchase, no payment, no sign-up needed to play or to win.
  • No follow needed. You do not have to subscribe, follow, like, comment or share anything to take part or to win.
  • Skill, not luck. The winner is the player with the best score on the quiz, as recorded by our server.
  • 18+ only. Prize: US $100. The exact final date will be posted here at least 7 days before it starts.

1. Organizer

The quiz and the championship are run by PrimeGlitch (the “Organizer”), the creator of the PrimeGlitch science channels. Contact: send a direct message to @primeglitchlab on Instagram.

This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, YouTube, Google, Instagram, Facebook, Meta, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Reddit or Cloudflare.

2. Who can enter

  1. You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live, if higher) when you play.
  2. You must live in a country or region where this kind of skill contest is legal. It is void where prohibited or restricted by law. People in countries or regions under comprehensive U.S., UN or EU sanctions cannot win a prize.
  3. The Organizer and members of the Organizer’s household cannot win.
  4. One person = one nickname. Using several nicknames, other people’s identities, bots or scripts is not allowed.

3. How to play

  1. Open quiz.pixoraimg.com and press Start. Each round has 10 science claims. You have 12 seconds to choose Myth or Truth for each one.
  2. Our server chooses the questions, records when each question appears and when you answer, and counts your score. Scores sent any other way are not accepted.
  3. After a round you can type a nickname (2–16 letters or numbers) to join the weekly leaderboard. You then get a claim code. Keep it (take a screenshot) — a winner must show it to claim a prize.
  4. Nicknames must not be offensive, misleading or someone else’s name. The Organizer may remove any nickname.

4. Weekly leaderboard

Each week runs from Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 (UTC). Your best round of the week counts. Players are ranked by score (higher is better), then by total answer time (faster is better), then by who reached that result first.

The top 3 nicknames of each week may be named in a PrimeGlitch Short or post. There is no money prize for the weekly leaderboard.

5. December Championship

  1. When: date to be announced. It will take place in December 2026. The exact start and end time (in UTC) will be posted on this page and on our channels at least 7 days in advance.
  2. How: during the championship window, the quiz uses a championship round. It may include new questions that were never used before. Only rounds played inside the window count.
  3. Winner: the eligible player with the highest score, recorded by our server during the window. Ties are broken by the lowest total answer time, then by who reached that result first. If a tie still remains, the tied players play one live tie-break round, watched by the Organizer on a video call.
  4. Prize: one prize of US $100 (or the same value in local currency). It is paid within 30 days after the winner is confirmed, by PayPal, Wise, Payoneer or a digital gift card of the winner’s choice, where available in the winner’s country. The prize cannot be transferred. Any taxes or fees in the winner’s country are the winner’s responsibility.
  5. Bigger prizes later: any future championship or larger prize will be announced with its own rules. Prizes never depend on subscribing, following, liking or sharing.

6. Claiming the prize

  1. After the championship ends, we post the winning nickname on this page and on our channels.
  2. The winner must send a direct message to @primeglitchlab on Instagram within 7 days with: the nickname, the claim code, and the country they live in.
  3. Before paying, the Organizer may ask the winner to confirm they are 18+ and to play one short live round on a video call, to make sure the result is real. The Organizer will never ask for passwords, card numbers or any payment from you.
  4. If the winner does not reply within 7 days, is not eligible, or cannot be verified, the prize goes to the next player in the ranking.

7. Fair play

Cheating, bots, scripts, changing the website or its requests, using many nicknames or devices to gain an advantage, or any other unfair method leads to disqualification. Rounds with impossibly fast answers are not ranked. The Organizer’s decisions about fair play and the final ranking are final.

8. Your data

We store only the nickname you type, your answers, score and answer times, and a hashed (not raw) internet address used to stop abuse. Round data is deleted after 2 days; leaderboard entries after 60 days. Visit counts use GoatCounter, without cookies. If you win, we use the details you send us only to verify you and pay the prize, and then delete them. The winning nickname is announced publicly.

9. Other

If a technical problem, fraud or a reason outside our control affects the fairness of the championship, the Organizer may change the date, extend or restart the window, or cancel it, and will explain why on this page. Changes to these rules are posted here with a new “last updated” date. These rules apply as far as the law of your country allows.
